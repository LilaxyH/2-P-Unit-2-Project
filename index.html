Top 10 Places to Visit in Taiwan

1. Taroko Gorge and Hualien

Some of the most beautiful natural scenery I've even vistied, highly recomend. You don't need to take a tour or anything, just pick a path and walk around. There are a lot well established trails, many have stairs and railings. There are also paved pedestrian walkways through the major attractions in the gorge if hiking is not your thing. Make sure to pick up some peanut mochi when you're there!

2. Taipei

The best way to visit Tiapei is to just walk around. The city has so many cute neighborhoods. I like the lay out of the city, it's not built on a grid like New York, there are lots of tiny side-streets and hidden corners to explore. It's also very easy to get around on a bike or by train, the public transportation is very clean and modern (at least compared to New York, lol)

A Breif History of Taiwan

Taiwan has a rich and interesting history. It is located off the coast of mainland China and has been claimed by many asian countries throught it's history. It was a part of China for hundreds of years. In the 1890s, Japan went to war with China and defeated them. As part of the treaty that ended the war, Taiwan became a part of the Japanese Empire. During this time, Taiwan developed a regional and cultural identity that was unique from both Japan and mainland China. After World War 2, Taiwan became a part of China again. After the Chinese civil war, the Nationalist Party fled to Taiwan and established the Taiwan Republic of China, sparking conflit with the people already living there. During the Cold War, the US provided military and economic aid to Taiwan, which helped them build a stable economy and eventually implement democratic reforms. Taiwan's sovereignty is still challenged by China, who claims that Taiwan is still a part of the country.
